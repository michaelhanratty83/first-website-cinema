WHERE WE ARE

OUR BUSINESS

We are a small independent cinema situated in the heart of the Castlereagh area. If coming from Belfast city centre, head in the East Belfast direction. Pass the bottom of the Cregagh Road take the next right onto the Castlereagh road. About one mile up the Castlereagh Road we are on the right hand side in a street called Ardpatrick Gardens. There is plenty of on street parking available.
